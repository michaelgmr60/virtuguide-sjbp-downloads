GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
before you go.

Do I need to print the markers?+

No. You can display a marker on a second phone, tablet or computer. Keep it still and avoid screen glare. You can also use Without Marker for Elementary, Preschool and SHS.

Can I scan the marker from the same phone?+

The AR camera needs to see the image outside the phone. Use a printout or another screen, or choose Without Marker instead.

Why does Android ask about installing an APK?+

The app is distributed through GitHub Releases rather than an app store. Your phone may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. Download the APK using the link on this page.

Is this a tour I can use in my browser?+

This website introduces the app and provides its downloads. The interactive campus tour, AR models and room views run inside the Android app.
