GOOD TO KNOW

A little help
before you explore.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. The virtual campus uses a phone screen, touch joystick and camera controls.

Can I use AR on any Android phone?

No. AR needs a device supported by Google ARCore. Virtual Tour remains available independently of AR support.

Can I explore without internet?

Download and install the APK first. Campus content is bundled with the app. Initial AR service installation or updates may require internet.

How do I scan a marker on my own phone?

Print it or display it on another screen. Keep the marker flat, fully visible and well lit while using the app camera.

Is this the school admissions website?

This website introduces the VirtuGuide thesis project and provides app downloads and markers. Contact the school directly for current admissions information.
